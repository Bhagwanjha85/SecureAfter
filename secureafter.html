Project Details & Problem Statement

What is SecureAfter?

SecureAfter is a secure digital platform designed to protect and manage sensitive personal documents and automatically share them with trusted nominees during emergency or critical situations. The project focuses on ensuring that important information remains accessible to authorized people when the original owner becomes inactive, unreachable, hospitalized, unconscious, missing, or deceased.

In today’s digital world, people store a large amount of important information such as medical records, bank documents, insurance policies, educational certificates, legal papers, investment details, passwords, and personal identity documents. However, during emergencies or after death, family members or trusted individuals often struggle to access these documents because they do not know where the data is stored or they do not have permission to access it. This creates serious problems related to healthcare, financial management, legal processes, insurance claims, and property handling.

The project also implements role-based access control, which means nominees can only access documents and information specifically authorized for them. This helps maintain privacy and security while still ensuring emergency accessibility. The platform combines cybersecurity, automation, cloud storage, emergency response systems, and secure authentication into one centralized solution.

SecureAfter is not just a cloud storage platform; it is an intelligent emergency management and secure digital inheritance system designed for real-world situations where timely access to information can save lives, reduce legal complications, and help families manage important responsibilities effectively.

Nowadays, people store many important documents in digital form such as medical reports, bank details, insurance papers, Government Identities, Property documents, Educational certificates, Investment records, legal documents, and personal identity documents etc...

To solve these problems, SecureAfter provides a secure emergency document management system. The platform allows users to securely store important documents, add verified nominees and automatically share authorized documents when emergency conditions are detected. The system uses encryption, authentication, activity monitoring, and role-based access control to ensure data security, privacy, and safe emergency access.

Result: Important digital information may become permanently inaccessible during emergencies.

Step-by-Step System Flowchart

1

Registration & Onboarding

User visits site → Signs up → Verifies email + mobile via secure OTP (tracked via OTPVerification with attempt limits) → Account created and User model marked as verified.

2

Dashboard Setup & Vault Initialization

Login → Upload encrypted documents (is_encrypted=True) → Add nominees (name, email, phone, relationship) → Nominees verified via OTP (ensuring contact details are valid).

3

Permission Configuration

Categorize documents (medical, financial, educational, personal, legal, insurance, etc.) → Set Nominee Access Levels (View Only, View & Download, Notes Only) → Write Emergency Instructions (medical consent, family guidance, secure notes).

4

Monitoring Phase (Dead Man's Switch)

Celery background workers run periodically → Checks user's Emergency Settings against next_check_in_due (customizable intervals like 7, 10, 15, 20, 30 days) → If overdue: system sends check-in ping alerts (Email/SMS) → Logs check-in status (on_time, late, missed) via CheckInRecord.

5

Emergency Trigger

Missed check-ins reach the configured threshold (default: 3 missed check-ins) → is_emergency_triggered flag is set to True → Secure EmergencyAccess tokens are generated → Verified nominees are notified instantly with access alerts. (Note: Nominees can also proactively submit a NomineeAccessRequest in case of sudden accidents, which triggers a grace-period approval workflow).

6

Nominee Access

Nominee clicks secure token link → Logs in with their linked verified email and an access verification code → Accesses only permitted documents and instructions based on RBAC → All actions (views, downloads) are strictly recorded in the AccessLog to ensure transparency.

Novelty of the Project

1. The "Are You Okay?" Check

Instead of relying on someone to report an emergency, SecureAfter checks on you automatically. If you don't respond to reminders, it safely assumes you need help and starts the emergency process without any human effort.

2. Smart "Who Sees What" Rules

Unlike other apps that share everything at once, we let you choose specific files for specific people. Your doctor only sees medical records, while your spouse sees financial details. You are in full control.

3. Strict Identity Checks

Even in an emergency, your data is locked tight. The people you trust (nominees) must verify their identity with a secure code (OTP) before opening any files, ensuring no stranger can access your private life.

Software Development Methodology (SDLC)

SecureAfter follows an Agile SDLC methodology divided into structured phases

Phase 1

Requirements & Planning (Week 1-2)

Gather core requirements, define role-based access logic, create structural wireframes, plan the relational database schema, and set up the project repository.

Phase 2

UI/UX Design & Prototyping (Week 3-4)

Design responsive mockups, define the styling system, map out the user journey for vault owners and nominees, and finalize the frontend architecture.

Phase 3

Frontend Interfaces (Week 5-7)

Develop Django templates and responsive views: Authentication portals, User Dashboard, secure Document Vault, and Nominee Management interface.

Phase 4

Backend & Security Development (Week 8-10)

Build the robust Django backend: Implement AES-256 encryption logic, document CRUD, access-control layers, and configure Celery background tasks for the Dead-Man Switch.

Phase 5

Integration & Testing (Week 11-12)

Integrate frontend with backend models, conduct rigorous unit and integration testing, perform security audits, and verify automated notification workflows.

Phase 6

Deployment & Review (Week 13-14)

Deploy to production environments, configure PostgreSQL databases, initialize Redis for Celery queues, set up secure cloud storage, and conduct final system review.

Project Limitations

Technical Limitations

  • Strict dependency on continuous Redis and Celery worker uptime for the Dead-Man Switch to function.
  • Zero-knowledge architecture means if a user loses their master passphrase, data cannot be recovered by admins.
  • File upload sizes are constrained by server memory limits and synchronous request timeouts in Django.
  • No offline functionality; document decryption and access requires a constant server connection.
  • Notification delivery is entirely dependent on third-party API reliability (Twilio/SendGrid).

Operational Limitations

  • The system infers status through digital inactivity; it cannot detect physical emergencies in real-time.
  • Users taking extended offline vacations must manually pause the switch to avoid false-positive triggers.
  • The burden of maintaining accurate and updated nominee contact details lies entirely on the user.
  • OTP-based nominee verification confirms device access but cannot verify the physical identity of the person.

Legal & Ethical Limitations

  • Digital documents stored on the platform may still require physical notarization to be court-admissible.
  • SecureAfter acts only as a vault; it cannot resolve real-world legal disputes regarding asset inheritance.
  • Strict data privacy regulations (like GDPR) restrict how encrypted backups can be retained.
  • Different countries have vastly different laws regarding the validity of "digital wills" and inheritance.

FAQ & User Guide

What exactly is SecureAfter?

SecureAfter is an automated, high-security document vault. It safely encrypts your most critical documents and automatically transfers access to your verified nominees if you become permanently inactive or unreachable.

How does the "Dead-Man Switch" work?

You set a custom check-in interval (e.g., every 30 days). The system will email or SMS you to verify you are active. If you miss 3 consecutive check-ins, our automated background engine assumes an emergency and securely dispatches document access links to your nominees.

Can I temporarily pause the Dead-Man Switch?

Yes. If you are going on a vacation, traveling off-grid, or will be without internet access, you can activate "Vacation Mode" in your dashboard to temporarily pause check-ins and prevent false emergency triggers.

How is my data secured?

Documents are secured using AES-256 military-grade encryption before being saved to private cloud storage. Passwords are hashed using Django's PBKDF2 algorithm, and all network traffic is secured via strict HTTPS/TLS protocols.

Can SecureAfter administrators or hackers read my documents?

No. We use a zero-knowledge architecture. Your documents are encrypted using your master passphrase before they even reach our servers. We do not store your passphrase, which means we cannot decrypt your files even if we wanted to.

What happens if I lose my master passphrase?

Because of our strict zero-knowledge encryption, if you lose your master passphrase, your documents cannot be recovered by anyone. We highly recommend keeping a physical backup of your passphrase in a secure location, like a home safe.

Can my nominees view my documents right now?

No. Your nominees are only granted access after the emergency protocol has been fully triggered. Until that moment, your documents remain completely inaccessible to everyone but you.

How does Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) help me?

RBAC allows you to compartmentalize your life. You can assign medical records strictly to your doctor, bank details to your spouse, and your digital will to your lawyer. A nominee will only ever see the specific files you have explicitly granted them permission to view.

How are nominees verified before they get access?

When you add a nominee, the system sends an OTP (One-Time Password) to both their email and mobile number. They must verify both to become active. When an emergency is triggered, they will again need to verify their identity via OTP to unlock their access link.

What if my nominee changes their email or phone number?

It is your responsibility to keep your nominee's contact details up-to-date via your User Dashboard. If their contact information is outdated when an emergency triggers, they will not receive the access link. We send automated bi-annual reminders to help you verify these details.

Can I remove or change a nominee later?

Yes, you maintain full control over your vault. Through your dashboard, you can revoke a nominee's access, delete them entirely, or reassign their document permissions at any time.

What types of documents can I upload?

You can upload any critical file, including medical records, financial statements, property deeds, crypto wallet recovery phrases, insurance policies, and digital wills. Standard formats like PDF, JPG, PNG, and DOCX are supported.

Is there a file size limit for uploads?

Yes. Depending on your subscription tier (Free vs. Premium), individual file uploads are capped (e.g., 10MB per file for Free users). This ensures fast, synchronous encryption and upload speeds to our AWS S3 buckets.

Can I update a document after it is uploaded?

Yes. You can log into your vault, delete an outdated document, and upload the newest version. You can then instantly update which nominees have permission to view the new file.

What happens if I just forget to check in?

To prevent false alarms, the system requires 3 consecutive missed check-ins across multiple days before triggering. Furthermore, if the switch is accidentally triggered, you can log in immediately to revoke access links before nominees view the files.

How exactly are nominees notified during an emergency?

Once the 3-strike check-in failure occurs, our background workers automatically dispatch secure alerts via Email and SMS to your verified nominees. These alerts contain a secure, time-sensitive link to access the specific documents you assigned to them.

Is SecureAfter legal to use, and are digital wills valid?

SecureAfter complies with data privacy laws like GDPR and the IT Act 2000. However, laws regarding the legal validity of "digital wills" vary wildly by country. We recommend consulting a legal professional to ensure your digital documents meet local notarization requirements.

What if I decide to delete my account?

We respect your Right to be Forgotten. If you delete your account, your encrypted files are permanently wiped from our storage buckets, and all personal metadata is scrubbed from our PostgreSQL databases following a 30-day grace period.
