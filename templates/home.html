FAQ & User Guide

Common questions about SecureAfter

Everything you need to know about our document vault, security measures, and automated emergency triggers.

General / Concept
What exactly is SecureAfter?
SecureAfter is an automated, high-security document vault. It safely encrypts your most critical documents and automatically transfers access to your verified nominees if you become permanently inactive or unreachable.
How does the "Dead-Man Switch" work?
You set a custom check-in interval (e.g., every 30 days). The system will email or SMS you to verify you are active. If you miss 3 consecutive check-ins, our automated background engine assumes an emergency and securely dispatches document access links to your nominees.
Can I temporarily pause the Dead-Man Switch?
Yes. If you are going on a vacation, traveling off-grid, or will be without internet access, you can activate "Vacation Mode" in your dashboard to temporarily pause check-ins and prevent false emergency triggers.
Security & Privacy
How is my data secured?
Documents are secured using AES-256 military-grade encryption before being saved to private cloud storage. Passwords are hashed using Django's PBKDF2 algorithm, and all network traffic is secured via strict HTTPS/TLS protocols.
Can SecureAfter administrators or hackers read my documents?
No. We use a zero-knowledge architecture. Your documents are encrypted using your master passphrase before they even reach our servers. We do not store your passphrase, which means we cannot decrypt your files even if we wanted to.
What happens if I lose my master passphrase?
Because of our strict zero-knowledge encryption, if you lose your master passphrase, your documents cannot be recovered by anyone. We highly recommend keeping a physical backup of your passphrase in a secure location, like a home safe.
Nominee & Access Control
Can my nominees view my documents right now?
No. Your nominees are only granted access after the emergency protocol has been fully triggered. Until that moment, your documents remain completely inaccessible to everyone but you.
How does Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) help me?
RBAC allows you to compartmentalize your life. You can assign medical records strictly to your doctor, bank details to your spouse, and your digital will to your lawyer. A nominee will only ever see the specific files you have explicitly granted them permission to view.
How are nominees verified before they get access?
When you add a nominee, the system sends an OTP (One-Time Password) to both their email and mobile number. They must verify both to become active. When an emergency is triggered, they will again need to verify their identity via OTP to unlock their access link.
What if my nominee changes their email or phone number?
It is your responsibility to keep your nominee's contact details up-to-date via your User Dashboard. If their contact information is outdated when an emergency triggers, they will not receive the access link. We send automated bi-annual reminders to help you verify these details.
Can I remove or change a nominee later?
Yes, you maintain full control over your vault. Through your dashboard, you can revoke a nominee's access, delete them entirely, or reassign their document permissions at any time.
Document Management
What types of documents can I upload?
You can upload any critical file, including medical records, financial statements, property deeds, crypto wallet recovery phrases, insurance policies, and digital wills. Standard formats like PDF, JPG, PNG, and DOCX are supported.
Is there a file size limit for uploads?
Yes. Depending on your subscription tier (Free vs. Premium), individual file uploads are capped (e.g., 10MB per file for Free users). This ensures fast, synchronous encryption and upload speeds to our AWS S3 buckets.
Can I update a document after it is uploaded?
Yes. You can log into your vault, delete an outdated document, and upload the newest version. You can then instantly update which nominees have permission to view the new file.
Triggers & Legal
What happens if I just forget to check in?
To prevent false alarms, the system requires 3 consecutive missed check-ins across multiple days before triggering. Furthermore, if the switch is accidentally triggered, you can log in immediately to revoke access links before nominees view the files.
How exactly are nominees notified during an emergency?
Once the 3-strike check-in failure occurs, our background workers automatically dispatch secure alerts via Email and SMS to your verified nominees. These alerts contain a secure, time-sensitive link to access the specific documents you assigned to them.
Is SecureAfter legal to use, and are digital wills valid?
SecureAfter complies with data privacy laws like GDPR and the IT Act 2000. However, laws regarding the legal validity of "digital wills" vary wildly by country. We recommend consulting a legal professional to ensure your digital documents meet local notarization requirements.
What if I decide to delete my account?
We respect your Right to be Forgotten. If you delete your account, your encrypted files are permanently wiped from our storage buckets, and all personal metadata is scrubbed from our PostgreSQL databases following a 30-day grace period.
